Dan & Athena

Built on Loyalty.
Inspired by Athena.

HaymanTech isn't just an IT company. It is a tribute to the most loyal companion I ever knew.

For 9 years, Athena was my shadow. She saw me through career changes, late nights studying engineering, and the ups and downs of life. She didn't care about servers or code—she cared about showing up.

When I decided to start this business, I looked at the IT industry and saw a lot of complexity, but very little connection. I wanted to build something that reflected the values Athena taught me: Unwavering loyalty, patience, and being there when it matters.

That is why she is in the logo. She reminds me every day that my job isn't just to fix computers—it's to be a reliable companion to the people who use them.

About Me (The Human)

Before IT, I spent 15 years managing busy hotels like The Rock Hotel and Brew and Bite Collective. I learned that technical skills are useless if you can't communicate with people.

I later pivoted to Enterprise Engineering, working with major MSPs to build secure networks and manage cloud migrations. I loved the tech, but I missed the human connection of hospitality.

HaymanTech is the best of both worlds. I bring enterprise-grade tools to your home or small business, but I deliver them with the warmth of a local friend.
