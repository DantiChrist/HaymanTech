Frequently Asked Questions

What if you can't fix the problem?
I operate on a strictly No Fix, No Fee basis. If I investigate the issue and cannot offer a solution or repair strategy, you will not be charged a cent. This guarantee is in addition to your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
Do you charge for travel time?
For my core areas (Macgregor, Sunnybank, Mt Gravatt, Eight Mile Plains), there is $0 travel fee. If you are further out (e.g., Redlands or Logan), a small fee ($20-$40) may apply to cover fuel and time. This will always be agreed upon before I arrive.
Is the Remote Support secure?
Yes. I use RustDesk, which is encrypted. You must approve the connection, you can watch everything I do, and once we disconnect, I cannot access your computer again without your permission.
